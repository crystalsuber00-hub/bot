## 1. Services
Designer will design and build a website for Client ("the Site"), including:

## 4. Client responsibilities
Client will provide, within [7] days of signing: business details, services, hours, photos, logo (if any), and any text Client wants on the Site. Client confirms it owns or has permission to use everything it provides, and that the information is accurate.

## 5. Domain name
Client will register the domain in Client's own name and keep it renewed. Client owns the domain at all times. Domain fees are paid by Client to the registrar and are not included in this Agreement.

## 6. Ownership
Client owns its content (text, photos, logo) at all times. After Client pays the setup fee in full, Client owns the final Site design and files and may request a copy of them at any time. Designer may display the Site in Designer's portfolio unless Client objects in writing.

## 7. Cancellation
- **Before work starts:** Client may cancel for a full refund of the deposit.
- **After work starts:** the deposit is non-refundable, as it covers work already done.
- **Monthly plan:** either party may cancel with [30] days' written notice (email is fine). After cancellation Designer will provide the Site files so Client can move them to another host.

Designer will build the Site with care but does not guarantee search engine rankings, website traffic, or business results. The Site is provided as is, and Designer is not responsible for outages caused by third-party hosting, domain or internet providers beyond Designer's control, though Designer will work in good faith to restore service.
